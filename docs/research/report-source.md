# TIMMY research report source

This is the canonical internal synthesis used to derive RFC 0001, the README,
and the public source ledger. Preserve uncertainty here instead of silently
upgrading ideas into implemented claims.

## Thesis

Generated-music evaluation is claim testing, not scalar scoring. Technical
integrity, semantic alignment, aesthetics, musical structure, editing,
distribution coverage, creative workflow, and listener preference require
different populations and protocols.

## Implemented after source verification

- KAD over user-supplied pinned embeddings, matching `kadtk`'s estimator.
- Ordered perturbation analysis with Kendall tau-b and explicit ties.
- MuseCPEval 0.3.0 thin adapter for five context-preservation facets.
- Matched prompt/seed long-form manifests at 30 s, 2 m, 4 m, and 8 m.
- Existing windowed alignment/aesthetics, temporal diagnostics, distribution
  measures, and blinded A/B studies retained as separate evidence.

## Not yet implemented

- MAD's MERT/MAUVE pipeline and its four full data-generation protocols.
- Region-aware edit instruction success and boundary seam perception.
- Vocal PER after source separation and ASR.
- Long-form degradation analysis and musical-form qualification on the
  implemented matched 30 s/2 m/4 m/8 m protocol.
- Workflow/agency event logging, C2PA inspection, memorization, and cultural
  bias protocols.

## Research corrections made

- Edit-context preservation is not an unoccupied niche: MuseCPBench and
  MuseCPEval now provide an explicit framework and implementation.
- KAD may be negative under its unbiased finite-sample estimator.
- A Fréchet distance over arbitrary embeddings must not be called FAD.
- C2PA provenance is not proof of truth or human authorship.
- TIMMY's current A/B study is not MUSHRA.
